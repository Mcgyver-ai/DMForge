# The Solo Maker Profile: Legal, Financial & Operational Baseline

**Identity:** Operating as a UK Sole Trader (Tibor Rames) building a portfolio of B2B SaaS and operational tools (Invoice Rescue, DM Forge, Mail-Wise).
**Structure:** As a sole trader, the business operates with unlimited personal liability, making strict adherence to regulatory boundaries, insurance, and compliance critical.

## 1. Regulatory Boundaries & Compliance

- **FCA Perimeter (Debt Collection):** The business acts strictly as a first-party credit-control service. It remains outside the FCA's regulated "debt-collecting" perimeter (Article 39F) because it chases ordinary commercial B2B trade invoices, not debts arising from regulated consumer credit or consumer hire agreements.
- **Marketing & ASA/CAP Code:** All marketing communications comply with CAP Code rule 3.1. General confidence ("puffery") is permitted, but specific factual, statistical, or cost-comparison claims used in outreach must be citable and substantiated.
- **AML Regulations:** The business does not currently fall under the nine HMRC-supervised sectors for Money Laundering Regulations 2017, as it is not a payment service provider or regulated credit business.

## 2. Financial & Tax Operations

- **HMRC & Tax:** Registered for Self Assessment, triggered by exceeding £1,000 in gross trading income per tax year.
- **Record Retention:** Financial and business records are kept for at least 5 years after the 31 January Self Assessment submission deadline of the relevant tax year.
- **Banking:** A dedicated business bank account is used to cleanly receive Stripe payouts and manage business expenses, separating them entirely from personal finances.

## 3. Legal Protection & Contracts

- **Insurance:** Due to the financial nature of the services, Professional Indemnity Insurance (PII) is the primary required coverage to protect against claims of financial loss caused by the service. Employers' liability is not required as there are no employees.
- **B2B Service Agreements:** Paid client tiers (e.g., Engine, Operator) are governed by formal service contracts. These contracts legally enforce the 3-month minimum terms (which Stripe does not enforce natively) and outline liability limits, scope, and payment terms.
- **Brand Protection:** UK IPO Trademark registration (e.g., in Class 36 or 35) is the standard path to legally protect active brand names like "Invoice Rescue" as they gain commercial traction.

## 4. Website & Data Protection Requirements

- **Transparency:** All public-facing websites must clearly display the true legal name (Tibor Rames) and an address where legal documents can be served.
- **Data Protection:** The business is registered with the ICO (Tier 1 micro organisation, £52/year) for processing personal data.
- **Complaints Process:** In compliance with the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (effective 19 June 2026), all products maintain a formal, accessible data protection complaints process that acknowledges receipts within 30 days.
- **Web Policies:** Websites enforce strict UK GDPR/PECR compliance, including mandatory Privacy Policies, Terms & Conditions, and Cookie consent banners with no pre-ticked boxes for non-essential tracking.
